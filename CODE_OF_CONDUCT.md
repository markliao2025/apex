# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for different viewpoints, constructive
feedback, accountability, and focus on the community's users.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults,
personal or political attacks, harassment, publishing private information without
permission, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this code. Reports should use GitHub private vulnerability
reporting for the repository and begin the report title with `Conduct:`. Reports will
be handled privately and fairly.

Consequences may include correction, warning, temporary exclusion, or permanent
exclusion, based on impact and repeated behavior.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project. It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
